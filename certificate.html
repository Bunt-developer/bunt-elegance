The Art of Authenticity

  • Artwork title, medium and dimensions
  • Edition number within a fixed, never-extended run, or “1 of 1” for originals
  • Artist / brand attribution to the Bunt Elegance Studio
  • Date of completion and of issue
  • Signature of the founder and an embossed studio stamp
  • Authenticity information — a unique certificate number linked to the Studio register
