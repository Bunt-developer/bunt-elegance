What your certificate records

  • Artwork name, medium, materials and dimensions
  • Edition number within a fixed, never-extended run — or “1 of 1” for originals
  • Artist / brand attribution to the Bunt Elegance studio
  • Date of completion and of issue
  • Signature of the founder and an embossed studio seal
  • Authenticity information — a unique certificate number linked to the studio register

QR & NFC verification

Each certificate carries a QR code leading to this page. Where applicable, an NFC tag is embedded discreetly in the artwork or its certificate — tap it with a phone to open its record.

Eligibility

Certificates accompany all originals, limited editions and private commissions. Open-edition works include a studio authenticity card.
