The Founders

Anand & Jeni Bhadja

Bunt Elegance began with a conviction shared by its two founders: that a single meaningful artwork can define a space more than anything else in it.

Anand and Jeni set out to build an art house rather than a décor label — one devoted to statement pieces with depth, material honesty and a story worth telling. Every work is conceived, crafted and finished under their direction, and every collector is welcomed personally.
