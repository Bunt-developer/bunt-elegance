Returns & refunds

We do not accept returns unless the artwork arrives damaged. Any damage must be reported within 72 hours of delivery. A continuous unboxing video must start with the package completely sealed and clearly show the entire unboxing process, including the damaged artwork. Refunds will be considered only after reviewing the video and verifying the damage. Please email your order details, along with the unboxing video and clear photos of the damage, to buntelegance@gmail.com within 72 hours.
