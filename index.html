02 — The Bunt Elegance Philosophy

Art beyond decoration

Art is not simply something placed on a wall. It shapes the character of a space — how it feels to walk in, how long you stay, what you remember.

We create statement pieces with the permanence of architecture and the intimacy of a personal story. Every work begins with a question: what should this room say about the people who live in it? The answer is sculpted by hand, layer by layer, in materials that change with the light.

06 — Craftsmanship

Crafted with intention

Nothing is printed. Every ridge, edge and gilded line is made by hand — and then examined again, under raking light, before it is allowed to leave the studio.

i.

Material

Archival mediums, mineral pigments, natural aggregates and metallic leaf — chosen for depth, longevity and the way they hold light.

ii.

Texture

Surfaces are sculpted in successive layers over days, allowing each to cure before the next is shaped.

iii.

Finishing

Edges are refined, gilding is burnished and every work is sealed for protection without losing its tactile character.

iv.

Presentation

Hand-finished float frames, hanging hardware fitted and levelled, and packaging designed as part of the experience.

07 — The Unboxing

An arrival worth remembering

Your artwork arrives as it should — protected, considered and revealed in sequence. Seven layers, each with a purpose, from the rigid outer box to a handwritten note.

    The Box

    A rigid black presentation box, embossed with the emblem and sealed by hand.

  2. 02

    Black Tissue

    Folded layers of black tissue, closed with a bronze seal.

  3. 03

    Artwork Sleeve

    A soft, acid-free sleeve protecting the surface and frame.

  4. 04

    Art Care Card

    Illustrated guidance for keeping the surface and frame pristine.

  5. 05

    Certificate of Authenticity

    Numbered, signed and verifiable — the work’s permanent record.

  6. 06

    Collector Card

    Your invitation to Collector Privilege and the Bunt Elegance registry.

  7. 07

    A handwritten note from the founders, closing the journey.

11 — About Bunt Elegance

Two founders. One conviction.

Bunt Elegance was founded by Anand Bhadja and Jeni Bhadja on a simple belief: a single, meaningful artwork can define a space more than anything else in it.

Together they built a contemporary Indian art house devoted to statement pieces for modern homes, hotels and workplaces — works rooted in craft, finished with restraint and made to be lived with for a lifetime.
