Shipping within India

Every artwork ships free of charge within India, fully insured from our studio to your door. Timelines are shown on each artwork page: ready-to-ship works are dispatched within 3–5 working days; made-to-order works within the stated production window.

Large works are delivered by specialist art handlers. We contact you before dispatch to arrange a convenient delivery time.

Packaging

Artworks travel in a custom crate or rigid box with corner guards and an acid-free sleeve, and are presented in the Bunt Elegance unboxing sequence: box, black tissue, artwork sleeve, art care card, Certificate of Authenticity, collector card and a thank-you card.

International delivery

We ship worldwide on request. International shipments are quoted individually based on size, destination and insurance. Import duties and local taxes are the responsibility of the recipient unless agreed otherwise.

Returns

  • Ready-to-ship works may be returned within 7 days of delivery, unused and in original packaging, with the certificate.
  • Made-to-order, personalised and commissioned works are final sale.
  • To start a return, email buntelegance@gmail.com with your order details. We will arrange collection.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 working days of the artwork reaching our studio in its original condition.

Transit damage

In the rare event of damage in transit, please photograph the packaging and artwork and contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We will repair, replace or refund the work in full.
