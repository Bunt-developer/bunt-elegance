Shipping details

Shipping within India

All our artworks are carefully packaged and shipped across India. Shipping charges apply and are calculated separately at checkout, before order confirmation.

Ready-to-ship artworks are dispatched within 7 to 14 working days. Delivery timelines may vary depending on the artwork and destination. Once your order is dispatched, you will receive shipping and tracking details to follow your delivery.

Large works are delivered by specialist art handlers. We contact you before dispatch to arrange a convenient delivery time.

Packaging

Artworks travel in a rigid box with corner guards and an acid-free sleeve, and are presented in the Bunt Elegance unboxing sequence: box, black tissue, artwork sleeve, art care card, Certificate of Authenticity, collector card and a thank-you card.

International delivery

We ship worldwide upon request. International shipments are quoted individually based on size and destination. Import duties and local taxes are the responsibility of the recipient unless agreed otherwise.
